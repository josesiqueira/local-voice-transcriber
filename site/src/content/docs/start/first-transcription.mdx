## Transcribe a voice note

<Steps>

1. Open [WhatsApp Web](https://web.whatsapp.com/) in Google Chrome. If it was already open when you loaded the extension, reload the tab.

2. Open a chat with a voice note. A small **T** button appears under each voice note and audio message.

3. Press **T**. The button is disabled and **Transcribing...** appears next to it.

    To capture the audio, the extension usually presses the note's own play button with the sound muted, then pauses and rewinds the note. You may see the play button change for a moment.

4. When the text is ready, the button disappears and a caption shows under the note: the transcript, a language tag such as **EN**, and a **Copy** link. **Copy** puts the text on your clipboard.

</Steps>

## Check where it ran

Hold the pointer over the language tag. A tooltip shows the device and the number of seconds the local program took, in the form `cuda, N s`.

- `cuda` means the GPU was used.
- `cpu` means the GPU attempt failed and the CPU was used. If you expected the GPU, see [Runs on the CPU](/local-voice-transcriber/guides/troubleshooting/#runs-on-the-cpu-instead-of-the-gpu).

The time includes starting the program and loading the model. The first transcription after a reboot takes longer than the ones after it, because the model is read from disk.

## Check the cache

Reload the WhatsApp Web tab and open the same chat. The caption shows again under the note without the T button and without running the model. The text comes from the extension's storage on your computer. See [Privacy](/local-voice-transcriber/privacy/privacy/#where-data-is-stored) for what is stored and for how long.

## If something goes wrong

When a transcription fails, a short message replaces **Transcribing...** and the button changes to **Retry**. Each message is listed with its cause and fix in [Troubleshooting](/local-voice-transcriber/guides/troubleshooting/).

Next, you may want to [limit detection to your languages](/local-voice-transcriber/guides/languages/) in the extension's popup, or [verify the privacy claims yourself](/local-voice-transcriber/privacy/verify/).
